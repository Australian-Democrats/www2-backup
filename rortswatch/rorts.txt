NAME: NDIS fraud
JURISDICTION: Federal
CATEGORY: Fraud
COST: $3.7 billion
SEVERITY: 8
DESCRIPTION: About $3.7 billion a year — roughly 8.3% of the scheme's ~$52 billion annual cost — is lost to fraud and non-compliant payments, according to the head of the NDIS Fraud Fusion Taskforce. Much of the organised fraud is run by high-end crime groups operating from overseas. Unchecked rorting by some providers jeopardises the scheme's future and threatens its availability to those with legitimate needs.
ACTION: The figure combines intentional fraud and unintentional misuse — the taskforce has said there is no statistically valid way to separate the two.
